LIBRARY POLICY

Rules & Regulations

Please observe these rules to maintain a quiet, safe and productive learning environment.

01

Library Membership

Users should have valid university identification where required to access library services.

02

Silence and Conduct

Maintain silence and behave respectfully toward other library users and staff.

03

Care of Library Materials

Books, computers, furniture and other library property must be handled carefully.

04

Borrowing Materials

Borrowed materials must be returned within the period specified by the library.

05

Food and Drinks

Food and drinks should not be taken into areas where they could damage library materials or equipment.

06

Computer Facilities

Library computers and internet facilities should be used responsibly and for legitimate academic purposes.
