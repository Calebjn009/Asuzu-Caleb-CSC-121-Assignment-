ABOUT THE LIBRARY

Supporting Learning and Research

The Maduka University Library provides students, lecturers and researchers with access to books, journals, electronic resources, study spaces and other academic services.

Our goal is to support teaching, learning, research and lifelong learning by providing reliable information resources and a welcoming academic environment.

Library Services

  • Book lending and borrowing
  • Reference services
  • Research assistance
  • Internet access
  • Digital resources
  • Quiet study spaces
